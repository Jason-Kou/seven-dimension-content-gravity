# Original seven-point summary

The method originated from seven observations about compelling content:

1. Emotion travels faster than raw information.
2. Usefulness must be visible immediately.
3. Stories make abstract ideas easier to understand and remember.
4. A trending topic needs a distinct interpretation.
5. A stable author identity creates long-term trust.
6. A new format can break through a crowded topic.
7. Specific people, scenes, conflicts, and details create resonance.

The current framework deliberately changed these from seven simultaneous requirements into seven optional editorial lenses.
