# Framework quick reference

- Emotional tension: why should the reader stop now?
- Reader benefit: what can the reader understand, decide, or do?
- Story/case: is there a person, scene, conflict, experiment, or sequence?
- Unique angle: what explanatory frame is different?
- Author voice: why should this author write it?
- Format/expression: is this the best container for the information?
- Specificity: are there concrete names, numbers, dates, sources, and limits?

Choose 2-4. Facts, thesis, confidentiality, and copyright are always mandatory.
